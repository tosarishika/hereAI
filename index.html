HOW IT WORKS

A check-in in three steps

  1. Start the camera

    Allow camera access and stand alone in front of the lens.

  2. Enter your roll number

    Once one face is detected, enter your enrolled roll number.

  3. You're marked present

    We record the time and prevent duplicate check-ins today.

Face presence is not identity verification or a liveness check. Photos and face features are not saved. Your roll number is required for every check-in.
